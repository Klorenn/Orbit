How It Works

Connect. Post. Govern.

01

Connect your wallet

Sign-in with Ethereum. No emails, no passwords. Your wallet is your identity — across every report, proposal, and vote.

02

Verified by NFT

Hold the Orbit Ambassador NFT to post reports and proposals. Anyone can read — only members can shape the record.

03

Stored on Filecoin

Reports and evidence pinned to IPFS, paid for via Filecoin storage deals. We eat our own dog food.

Built With The Community

Aligned with the Constellation Program.

This forum is a contribution to the Filecoin Foundation's Constellation Program for modernizing governance. It complements Metropolis — where FIP signaling already lives — and fills the missing gap: a durable home for ambassador activity, event reporting, and open project debate.
